Create an official institutional image of the Methodius Institute.

==================================================
VISUAL UNIVERSE
==================================================

The image must belong to the exact same visual world as the Institute's publications, staff portraits and book covers.

Visual references:

- university annual reports
- institutional documentation
- official conference photography
- scientific organizations
- editorial magazine illustration

The image documents an event of great importance to the Institute.

The Institute treats ordinary events with excessive seriousness.

Humor must emerge entirely from the documented situation.

==================================================
COLOR PALETTE
==================================================

Use exclusively:

#F1ECE2
#E7E0D2
#1B2340
#5A5F72
#B5292C

No other colors.

No realistic object colors.

No realistic skin tones.

No yellow.

No sepia.

No archive tint.

No vintage treatment.

Maximum red usage:
5%

The image must visually match the Institute's publications.

==================================================
RENDERING
==================================================

Flat 2D editorial illustration.

Premium literary publishing aesthetic.

Stylized realism.

Painterly editorial rendering.

Strong directional lighting.

Large tonal shapes.

Minimal linework.

Soft atmospheric depth.

Readable faces.

Recognizable expressions.

No photorealism.

No simulated photography.

==================================================
COMPOSITION
==================================================

Formal institutional composition.

People pose deliberately.

Everyone appears convinced of the importance of the occasion.

Strong hierarchy.

Clear focal point.

Controlled composition.

No visual clutter.

Maximum 8 people.

Symmetrical compositions preferred.

confident

comedy reactions

cartoon humor

ATMOSPHERE
==================================================

The image should look like a perfectly serious institutional document.

The absurdity comes solely from what is being documented.

The viewer should initially believe they are looking at a real academic institution.

Only afterwards should the satire become apparent.

The image must feel like it was created by the same publisher, designer and art director who created the Institute's book covers.

--------------------------------------------------
READER ADDRESSING RULE
--------------------------------------------------

The guide series consistently addresses readers using:

DU

Never use:

Sie
Ihnen
Ihr
Ihre

When generating titles, subtitles, taglines or any reader-facing text,
use the informal German second person singular.

Examples:

Wie du bessere Entscheidungen triffst.
Nicht:
Wie Sie bessere Entscheidungen treffen.

This rule applies to the entire guide series and should be treated as a
permanent editorial style guideline.

--------------------------------------------------
TITLE BLOCK
--------------------------------------------------

The title is the primary visual element.

The title must occupy approximately
45–55% of the cover height.

The illustration supports the title.

The title should remain readable at thumbnail size.

The title should feel oversized and iconic.

Prefer:

2–4 title lines.

Avoid narrow line breaks.

Create a strong compact title block.

TITLE DOMINANCE RULE

The title block is the single largest element on the cover.

The title should visually occupy
at least twice the area of the illustration.

When viewed from a distance:

1. Title
2. Illustration
3. Subtitle
4. Author

must be perceived in exactly that order.

If there is a conflict between illustration size
and title size, reduce the illustration.

--------------------------------------------------
TYPOGRAPHY
--------------------------------------------------

Visual reference:

- Fraunces Black

Use tight line spacing.

Lines should visually lock together into a powerful typographic block.

Typography should dominate the cover.

--------------------------------------------------

Prefer:

- social interaction
- home office scene
- everyday absurdity
- visual irony

The viewer should understand the joke immediately.

POSTER CONTRAST RULE

The protagonist should be readable
as a silhouette before facial details become visible.

--------------------------------------------------
ILLUSTRATION RENDERING
--------------------------------------------------

ILLUSTRATION RENDERING

The illustration should be built primarily from
large graphic shadow shapes.

Think:

70% shape
20% silhouette
10% detail

NOT:

70% linework
20% detail
10% shadow

The illustration must remain immediately readable
at Amazon thumbnail size.

Visual references:

- The Economist cover illustrations
- Harvard Business Review artwork
- Mid-century advertising illustration
- Saul Bass poster composition
- Vintage corporate editorial illustration

Use:

- large navy shadow masses
- bold charcoal shapes
- simplified facial features
- simplified clothing folds
- simplified environments

Avoid:

- excessive hatching
- pen-and-ink rendering
- engraving aesthetics
- newspaper illustration style
- sketchbook aesthetics
- intricate detail

Shadows should define the image.

Lines should only support the shapes.

The illustration should feel sculpted from
light and shadow.

The result should resemble a premium editorial poster,
not a drawing.

CONTRAST RULE

Use strong contrast.

At least:

60–70% of the illustration should consist of
medium-to-dark values.

Avoid pale illustrations.

Avoid low-contrast scenes.

The focal character should contain
large navy shadow regions.

The illustration must remain recognizable
when reduced to 150px height.

SERIES ILLUSTRATION RULE

Every cover in the series should feel like it was
illustrated by the same artist.

Use:

- identical shadow treatment
- identical facial simplification
- identical shape language
- identical contrast level

The illustration style should become
a recognizable series signature.

Avoid:

- engraving style
- newspaper illustration style
- excessive line detail
- sketchbook style
- comic style
- photorealism

The illustration should feel sculpted through shape and shadow rather than linework.

Create artwork in the official visual style of the Methodius Institute.

--------------------------------------------------
VISUAL IDENTITY
--------------------------------------------------

Premium editorial illustration.

Intelligent.
Sophisticated.
Timeless.
Dryly humorous.
Academic but slightly absurd.

The artwork should feel like a lost illustration from a prestigious German magazine from an alternative version of the 1950s.

Visual references:

- The Economist cover illustrations
- Harvard Business Review artwork
- Mid-century editorial illustration
- Vintage German magazine illustrations
- Saul Bass graphic design
- 1950s corporate advertising art

Never look like:

- comic art
- caricature
- manga
- fantasy art
- AI concept art
- stock illustrations
- photorealism

--------------------------------------------------
COLOR PALETTE
--------------------------------------------------

Background:
#F1ECE2

Primary dark tone:
#1B2340

Secondary dark tone:
charcoal gray

Accent:
#B5292C

Allowed colors:

- cream
- navy
- charcoal
- warm gray
- muted red

Forbidden:

- bright blue
- green
- yellow
- orange
- purple
- neon colors

The image should feel almost monochrome.

Red should occupy no more than 5–10% of the image.

--------------------------------------------------
ILLUSTRATION PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------

The image should be built primarily from shapes and contrast.

Think:

70% shadow masses
20% silhouette
10% detail

NOT:

70% linework
20% detail
10% shadow

Use large shadow areas.

Use bold simplified forms.

Use strong silhouettes.

Keep details minimal.

The image must remain readable at thumbnail size.

--------------------------------------------------
RENDERING STYLE
--------------------------------------------------

Flat 2D artwork.

Mid-century editorial illustration.

Large navy shadow masses.

Strong contrast.

Minimal hatching.

Minimal linework.

Simplified facial features.

Simplified clothing folds.

Simplified environments.

The image should feel sculpted from light and shadow.

Visual weight should come from shapes, not outlines.

--------------------------------------------------
CONTRAST
--------------------------------------------------

High contrast.

At least 60–70% medium-to-dark values.

Avoid pale illustrations.

Avoid washed-out scenes.

The focal character should contain large dark shadow areas.

The image should remain recognizable when reduced to 150px height.

--------------------------------------------------
CHARACTER DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------

Characters should appear:

- intelligent
- competent
- composed
- self-confident
- slightly self-satisfied

Avoid exaggerated facial expressions.

Avoid cartoon reactions.

Avoid slapstick humor.

Humor should emerge from the situation.

--------------------------------------------------
COMPOSITION
--------------------------------------------------

Strong central focal point.

Clear hierarchy.

No clutter.

No unnecessary background elements.

Every object should support the story.

The scene should communicate its idea immediately.

--------------------------------------------------
ATMOSPHERE
--------------------------------------------------

Academic.

Institutional.

Editorial.

Slightly absurd.

Deadpan.

The image should look as if it illustrates a serious scientific article that happens to be based on questionable assumptions.

--------------------------------------------------
SERIES CONSISTENCY
--------------------------------------------------

Use the same visual language as:

- Methodius Institute guide covers
- Institute staff portraits
- Institute founding photographs
- Institute archive material

Every image should feel instantly recognizable as part of the same visual universe.

--------------------------------------------------
FINAL REQUIREMENT
--------------------------------------------------

The artwork should look like it was created by the same illustrator.

Consistent shadow treatment.
Consistent shape language.
Consistent contrast level.
Consistent facial simplification.
Consistent editorial aesthetic.

The result should resemble a premium illustrated feature in a respected 1950s intellectual magazine.

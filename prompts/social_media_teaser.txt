# DESIGN REFERENCE SYSTEM (MANDATORY)

The final graphic must visually match the supplied reference examples.

Use the references as the primary design language.

The goal is that different guidebooks appear as part of the same publishing campaign.

The supplied front cover must be used exactly as provided.

Do not redesign it.
Do not recreate it.
Do not modify typography.
Do not change colors.
Do not alter the illustration.
Do not reconstruct any cover elements.

Treat the cover as finished artwork.

---

# CANVAS

Format: 1:1

Size: 2048 × 2048 px

Background:
#FCFAF5

Warm ivory.

No gradients.
No texture.
No patterns.

Clean editorial appearance.

---

# COLOR SYSTEM

Primary Editorial Blue:
#1B2340

Use for:

- headline
- teaser copy
- author name
- decorative elements

Accent Red:
#B5292C

Use ONLY for:

- the most important word or phrase in the headline
- decorative divider line
- CTA

Maximum red usage:
15–20% of total text.

The design must remain predominantly blue.

---

# BOOK PLACEMENT

Place the supplied cover on the LEFT side.

Book height:
55–65% of canvas height.

Book occupies approximately:
30–35% of total canvas width.

Book angle:
4–8° perspective rotation.

Slim booklet appearance.

Spine width:
3–5 mm.

Apply:

- soft contact shadow
- subtle realistic drop shadow
- premium studio lighting

The book must clearly contrast against the background and remain visually distinct.

---

# TYPOGRAPHY

Headline font style:

Elegant editorial serif.

Similar characteristics to:

- Freight Display
- Chronicle Display
- Tiempos Headline
- Canela Display

Font weight:
Bold

Optical appearance:
700–800

Tracking:
slightly tight

Line height:
85–92%

Headline size:

Very large.

Headline block should occupy:

45–60% of the right-side area.

Headline must dominate the composition.

---

# HEADLINE STRUCTURE

Use 2–4 lines.

Preferred structure:

Line 1:
dark blue

Line 2:
dark blue

Line 3:
accent red

Example:

Mehr Muskeln.
Mehr
Probleme.

or

Du musst nicht
kompetent sein.
Nur kompetent
wirken.

The red phrase should contain:

- the contradiction
- the punchline
- the irony
- the reveal

Only one phrase should be red.

Never color more than 35% of the headline red.

---

# TEXT BLOCK POSITIONING

Headline begins:

approximately 58–62% from left edge.

Top edge:

18–24% from top.

Text aligned LEFT.

Never centered.
Never justified.

Maintain a strong rectangular text block.

---

# DECORATIVE DIVIDER

Place a thin horizontal red line between headline and teaser text.

Color:
#B5292C

Length:
90–110 px

Thickness:
4 px

Corner style:
square

Position:

40–60 px below headline.

---

# TEASER TEXT

Position:

below divider line.

Maximum:
3–5 lines.

Width:
28–35% of canvas width.

Font:

modern editorial sans serif.

Examples:

- Inter
- Suisse Int'l
- Neue Haas Grotesk
- Source Sans

Weight:
400–500

Color:
#1B2340

Font size:

28–38 px
(relative to a 2048 × 2048 canvas)

Line height:
140–150%

Must feel calm and intelligent.

Never promotional.

Never use marketing clichés.

---

# CTA

Position:

bottom right.

left

- Jetzt entdecken

- Mehr erfahren
- Zum Ratgeber

Font:

same as teaser text.

600

Letter spacing:
+180 to +250

Color:
#B5292C

22–28 px

Arrow thickness:
2 px

Arrow color:
#B5292C

16–24 px

---

# WHITESPACE

Extremely important.

At least:

40% of canvas should remain empty.

The design must feel:

- premium
- calm
- intellectual
- expensive

Never crowded.
Never busy.
Never advertising-like.

---

# VISUAL QUALITY TARGET

The result should resemble:

- a premium European publishing campaign
- a literary magazine advertisement
- a bestselling non-fiction launch from an established publishing house

Not:

- sales page
- online marketing ad
- self-help funnel
- clickbait graphic

The overall impression should be:

minimal,
intelligent,
ironic,
elegant,
highly readable,
and instantly recognizable as part of the same book series.

---

# CRITICAL COMPOSITION RULE

The visual hierarchy must always be:

1. Headline (largest element)
2. Book cover
3. Teaser text
4. CTA

The headline should immediately attract attention within one second of viewing.

The book cover supports the message.

The typography drives the design.

The overall layout should feel 70% typography and 30% product presentation.
